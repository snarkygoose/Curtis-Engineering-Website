Our Story

Four Generations, One Family, One Workshop

From a single set of hands in 1951 to a fourth-generation team still working the same trade today.

1951 – Today

Founded In Port Elizabeth. Still Family-Run Today.

Curtis Engineering was founded in November 1951 in North End, Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha), and has been fabricating and machining for Eastern Cape industry ever since. Seventy-five years on, the business is still owned and run by the same family — today by fourth-generation owner Garth Morris — and still operates from its roots in North End.

That continuity is the core of who we are. Each generation has learned the trade from the one before it, carrying forward decades of hands-on workshop knowledge while adding the equipment, processes and quality standards that modern industrial clients expect. The result is a business that combines old-school craftsmanship with current machining capability — and a level of trust that only comes from three-quarters of a century of doing the work.

That same standard is upheld today by a team of long-serving, hands-on craftsmen, including Wally Snelling and Alex Morris, whose experience shows in the quality and consistency of every job that leaves our workshop.
