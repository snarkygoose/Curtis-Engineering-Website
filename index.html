Who We Are

Old-School Craftsmanship. Modern Machining Capability.

Curtis Engineering has been fabricating and machining for Eastern Cape industry since November 1951. Now run by fourth-generation owner Garth Morris, we combine decades of accumulated know-how with the equipment and quality control that today's contracts demand.
